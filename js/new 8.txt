第一优先：Probability（概率）

最适合接在刚做完的 Statistics 后面，而且基本不用画图。可以分成：

Impossible / unlikely / even chance / likely / certain
Probability scale 0–1
Simple probability as a fraction
Favourable outcomes / total outcomes
Coins
Dice
Spinners（可以纯文字描述）
Coloured counters / balls in a bag
Complementary probability
Comparing probabilities
Experimental probability
Expected frequency
Two-step probability
With / without replacement
Probability reasoning and fairness

这个我认为是下一个最值得做的。

第二优先：2D & 3D Shapes

和Measurement分开，专门学习图形性质：

triangle types
quadrilateral types
regular / irregular polygons
sides / vertices / faces / edges
nets
prism / pyramid
cube / cuboid
symmetry
shape classification
properties and logic

大部分可以纯文字完成，例如：

A quadrilateral has four equal sides and four right angles. Name the shape.

第三优先：Coordinates and Transformations

可以做：

x-axis / y-axis
origin
four quadrants
read coordinates
plot coordinates
horizontal / vertical distance
translation
reflection
rotation
simple enlargement
missing vertex
coordinate patterns

不过这个主题有一部分有图会明显更好，可以先做纯文字坐标版本。



第五优先：Financial Mathematics / Money

虽然现在很多Topic里已经散布着money应用题，但还没有系统训练：

dollars and cents
making amounts
change
total cost
unit price
best buy
budget
discounts
simple percentage increase/decrease
profit / loss
tax
wages
simple interest（可以放高阶）
multi-step shopping reasoning

和Percentage有一些重合，所以可以更强调实际应用与决策。

第六优先：Patterns and Sequences

现在Whole Numbers和Algebra里都有一点，但还可以专门做：

additive patterns
multiplicative patterns
alternating patterns
missing terms

position-to-term
shape-free number patterns
tables of values
arithmetic sequences
reverse patterns
multi-rule patterns
pattern logic
第七优先：Graphs and Charts

这个就是刚才Statistics暂时没做的视觉部分：

pictograms

line graphs
pie charts
misleading graphs

但这个最好有图以后再做，我不会把它放在当前优先位置。
